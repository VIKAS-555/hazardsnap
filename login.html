Technical Roles & Contribution Tracks

Club governance model for the Department of CSE (AI & ML). Earn 100 points to auto-promote from Active Developer to Staff Contributor (maintain ≥60 pts). Leadership ranks (Core Maintainer & Lead Admin) are appointed manually and exempt from points.

4 COMMUNITY ROLES
Lead Administrator (Club Admin) LEAD ADMIN PASS

Root / UID 0. Highest tier in club administration and platform infrastructure. Operates under multi-admin governance (there can be multiple Lead Admins, but never less than one).

▸ Authority: Exclusive authority to manually promote members to Core Maintainer or Lead Admin, and demote any lower rank.
▸ Auto-System: Exempt from contribution points, auto-promotion, and auto-demotion.
Tier 4 • Platform Governance EXEMPT FROM DECAY
Core Maintainer CORE MAINTAINER PASS

Main Branch Authority. Oversees domain tracks (Competitive Programming, Robotics, AI & ML), coordinates technical project architecture, and reviews code submissions.

▸ Promotion: Appointed exclusively by Lead Administrators. Cannot be unlocked through automated contribution points.
▸ Auto-System: Exempt from contribution points, auto-promotion, and auto-demotion.
Tier 3 • Technical Leadership EXEMPT FROM DECAY
Staff Contributor STAFF CONTRIBUTOR PASS

Senior Peer Mentor. Guides junior developers, assists in workshops, and conducts initial code reviews across active repositories.

▸ Auto-Promote: Unlocked automatically when an Active Developer reaches 100+ merit points.
▸ Maintenance: Must maintain ≥60 points. Falling below 60 points triggers auto-demotion to Active Dev.
▸ System Ceiling: Auto-promotion strictly caps here. Advancement to higher tiers is by Lead Admin only.
Tier 2 • Senior Contributor MAINTAIN ≥60 PTS
Active Developer ACTIVE DEVELOPER PASS

Verified Student Member. Entry tier for CSE (AI & ML) undergraduates who claim their official Tech Club ID. Participates in hackathons, workshops, and team builds.

▸ Merit Points: +20 pts per verified peer referral • +15 pts per workshop RSVP • +25 pts per project showcase.
▸ Auto-Promote: Reaching 100 points automatically promotes you to Staff Contributor rank!
Tier 1 • Member Registry TARGET: 100 PTS
